# Human IPA phoneme recordings

Source: [Yun-lab7/ipa-sound-lab](https://github.com/Yun-lab7/ipa-sound-lab) audio directory.
The recordings are human pronunciations from Wikimedia Commons. The page code is MIT;
the audio files keep their own licenses (mostly CC BY-SA 3.0, one public domain).

Local modifications: OGG converted to mono 22,050 Hz WAV, silence trimmed, capped at 0.70 s,
faded, and peak-normalized. Diphthongs are composed at runtime from two of these vowel recordings.
